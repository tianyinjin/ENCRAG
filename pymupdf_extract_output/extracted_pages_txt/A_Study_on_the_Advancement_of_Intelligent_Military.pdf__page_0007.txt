2) Observation, Action, Reward
Observations provided to the agent are categorized into image
data and vector data as outlined in Table 2.
TABLE 2. Types of observational inputs for reinforcement learning agent
Type of Observation
Description
Image Data
Bounding box included image
Vector Data
Detection confidence level
Camera tilt angle
Drone rotation angle
Drone x-axis coordinate
Drone z-axis coordinate
Drone x-axis velocity
Drone z-axis velocity
* Drone y-axis altitude controlled by PID
Image data comprises 84x84x3 images with bounding
boxes marked in purple as illustrated in Figure 8. Vector
data is comprised of seven variables, detailed as follows.
The detection confidence level spans from 0% to 100%. The
camera tilt angle refers to the angular position of the camera
attached underneath the drone in relation to its tilting around
the x-axis. The drone rotation angle represents the angular
rotation of the drone itself around the y-axis. The units for the
drone’s x and z-axis coordinates and velocities are in meters
(m) and meters per second (m/s), respectively. Since the
drone’s altitude, corresponding to the y-axis, is autonomously
regulated by PID control, the agent is not required to manage
it, hence it is omitted from the observations.
