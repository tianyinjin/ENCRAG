CHARTS 
Charts represent the sales breakdown by business segment (i.e. branches or divisions as presented by 
the company) and geographical region. 
MAIN PROGRAMMES, COOPERATIONS AND EXPORT PARTNERSHIPS
The “Main programmes, cooperations and export partnerships” section presents the main programmes 
highlighted by companies in their public media releases (excluding upstream study programmes). 
Partnerships for each programme are also listed, without further details. These partnerships can 
take several forms: co-contractorship, co-development or co-production agreements, subcontracting 
agreements (for tier-1 equipment suppliers considered as “strategic partners”), marketing agreements, 
joint ventures (JV), or partnerships with foreign companies as part of offset agreements.
Due to the very concise format of the notebook, this section cannot claim to be exhaustive.
