OPERATIONAL POLICY/ PROCEDURE 
B. Endorsements of Candidates 
It is the policy of the SAENA not to endorse any candidates for public 
office in any manner, either verbally or in writing. This policy extends to 
the actions of representatives of the SAENA, when these individuals are 
acting on behalf of, or are otherwise representing, the SAENA. 
 
C. Prohibited Use of State Council Assets and Resources 
It is the policy of the SAENA that no assets or human resources of said 
Chapter will be utilized for political activities, as defined as above. This 
prohibition extends to the use of SAENA assets or human resources in 
support of political activities are engaged in personally by board 
members or any other representative of the SAENA. While there is no 
prohibition against these individuals engaging in political activities 
personally (on their own time, and without representing that they are 
acting on behalf of the SAENA), these individuals must at all times be 
aware that SAENA resources cannot at any time be utilized in support of 
political activities.
