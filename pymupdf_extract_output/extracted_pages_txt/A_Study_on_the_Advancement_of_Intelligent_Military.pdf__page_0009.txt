D. COMPREHENSIVE APPLICATION AND VALIDATION OF
TECHNOLOGIES
Finally, we were able to confirm that the series of processes,
which include using rule-based waypoints for reconnaissance
and tracking upon enemy detection, followed by activating
reinforcement learning to enhance detection confidence level,
proceeded smoothly. The following explains how the process
was implemented and verified through Figure 12. In Figure
12(a), the waypoint setup is indicated in yellow, and the
drone and tank hidden in a structure are marked with sky-
blue circles. Figure 12(b) shows the view of the drone in the
sky from the perspective of an enemy on the ground. Figure
12(c) depicts the start of the drone’s reconnaissance mission.
The text in the top right of the figure indicates the altitude
and step count, a concept of discrete time. Here, it’s evident
that the drone maintains an altitude of about 150 meters. As
the drone patrols the designated waypoints, it discovers a
tank as shown in Figure 12(d). Then, as seen in Figure 12(e),
the drone moves directly to track the tank, positioning it at
the center of the drone camera’s image. Up to this point,
before the activation of the reinforcement learning neural
network, the camera continuously faces directly downward.
Once the tank is centered in the drone’s camera view, the
confidence level is checked to see if it exceeds the arbitrarily
set benchmark of 90% specifically designated for this study.
Here, the confidence level is below 90%, so the reinforcement
learning neural network is activated as shown in Figure 12(f).
This allows the drone to move and adjust the camera angle
towards the tank, securing visibility and ultimately increasing
the confidence level to over 90%.

IV. CONCLUSION
This paper offers an engineering perspective within the mil-
itary field on using drones with object detection capabilities
for military purposes. This is explored through a case study
based on specific scenarios. When thinking about using smart
drones in the military, it’s important to consider factors such
as the complexity of the environment, efforts by enemies to
interfere, lack of data, the need for stable operation, and the
provision of information for making attack decisions. The
environmental complexity in this scenario is highlighted by
the task of identifying enemies concealed under structures
or trees in varied terrains, including urban and mountainous
regions. In fulfilling this mission, the drone is programmed
to maintain a certain altitude to enhance the detection of
hidden enemies and to safeguard against unforeseen threats.
