2.1 
Radar detection 
The main goals of testing and calculation were to determine the targets radar cross-section (RCS) and 
afterward the possibilities of their detection, localisation, tracking and identification 
Drones were tested in several electromagnetic spectrum bands in accordance with [7]: radar (RL), radio-
frequency (RF), electro-optical (EO), infrared (IR), and acoustic. In some cases, drones were tested in a 
multispectral field through “combined sensors”. 
The first phase of RL experiments included the simulation of objects and their RCS in SuperNEC software. 
The software was used for the 3D modelling of objects (see Fig. 2.) and their subsequent analysis from a 
theoretical RCS point of view. The simulation principle lies in the power gain measurement (Fig. 3.) and 
subsequent RCS calculation.
