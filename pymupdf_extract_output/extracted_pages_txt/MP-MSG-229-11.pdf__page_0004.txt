In MUM-T teams, human and unmanned agents exhibit fundamentally different characteristics that influence 
collaboration and coordination. Human cognition is highly flexible but also subject to variability due to 
stress, workload, experience, and other human factors, which can impact decision-making and situational 
awareness. In contrast, unmanned agents have cognition encoded in algorithms, resulting in consistent 
performance that depends primarily on sensor input and the quality of their reasoning models. Humans rely 
on perception through biological senses, which can be augmented but remain limited, whereas unmanned 
systems utilize algorithmic perception to process data from multiple sensors, enabling faster and more 
precise environmental awareness. Decision-making in humans is adaptive and generalizable, often guided by
