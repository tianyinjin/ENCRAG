Sirs, Ladies and Gentlemen, 
 
After 3 years in the CJOC, my NATO career is slowly drawing to and end (this chapter at least), and I will spend 
my last working day here on the 27 Apr (before I go through the joyful experience of out-processing and 
relocation).  From BRUNSSUM, I will move onto Northwood where I will be working on the national side of the 
establishment back in my normal maritime environment.  
 
Before I leave.... 
I would be honored if you could find time to join me for luncheon at 12:00 on the 27 April in the CJOC Conf 
Room. 
 
To help with my planning (how much Marmite to buy etc), please confirm your availability for this time. 
 
For those who have not yet mastered making coffee, this means that you only have 17 working days in which to 
milk my coffee making expertise.  Please note that due to my hectic schedule, Coffee machine training is now by 
appointment only ( I will make time, I would hate to leave here worrying that my former colleagues may 
dehydrate). 
 
If you have any questions regarding the information above, please do not hesitate to contact me. 
 
Regards 
 
CPO(AWT) Pete S.
