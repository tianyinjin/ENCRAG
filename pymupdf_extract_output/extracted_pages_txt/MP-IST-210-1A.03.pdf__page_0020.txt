3.2.2 
Establishing Simulation Environments for Data Collection & Dynamic Testing 
While a fixed set of still images and video data may be useful to establish a baseline understanding of CV 
system performance when given these inputs, clear limitations persist. These include the inability to: (a) 
evaluate counter-drone systems on dynamic adversary drone maneuvers and scenarios (e.g. multi-drone 
attack formations), (b) account for the full range and representation of realistic environmental phenomena in 
which they are expected to operate, where data is insufficient, (c) reproduce the idiosyncratic sensor 
behaviors where real data may not exist for sensors yet to be deployed, and (d) fully appreciate the terrain 
limitations of each sensor mode, especially including line-of-sight issues for optical cameras or terrain-
masking issues for IR or LIDAR sensors. Moreover, where the dataset distribution is not sufficient to support 
a representative weightage of the data for the actual use in the operational design domain, performance 
metrics on the dataset cannot be considered transferable to the actual usage scenario. For example, a drone 
dataset consisting of 80% of bright light, clear sky, no occlusion images/videos would not provide sufficient 
assurance of consistent CV system performance under an actual usage scenario in a winter setting, with 60% 
rainy conditions.
