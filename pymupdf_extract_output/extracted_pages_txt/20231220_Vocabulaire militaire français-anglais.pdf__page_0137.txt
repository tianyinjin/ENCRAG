Page 137 
 
The Armed Forces 
 
Questions & Answers 
 
 
 
How old do I have to be to join the Services?  
Officers 17 years, Other Ranks 16 years. 
 
What sort of qualifications do you need? 
Officers: 2 GCE 'A' Levels (or equivalent), and 5 GCSEs (or equivalent) with English & Maths 
as mandatory subjects. Other Ranks need to pass a recruit selection test. 
 
Can women be fighter pilots? 
Yes. There are already female fixed wing pilots106 in the RAF. 
 
I have heard that women can’t join the Infantry. Why? 
All cap-badged posts107 in the Regular Infantry [= tous les postes de combattants dans 
l’infanterie] (and Household Cavalry/Royal Armoured Corps) are presently closed to women on 
the grounds of combat effectiveness. The Government is wholly committed to maximising 
employment opportunities for women in the Armed Forces and, to this end the Army is 
currently conducting a study into the effects of gender upon combat effectiveness, which is 
due to report to Ministers in 2001. 
 
How many women and people from ethnic minorities are already employed in the 
Services? 
At 1 October 1999 (latest figures available) there were 2,281 ethnic minority personnel in the 
Armed Forces, representing 1.1% of the total strength. 
At 1 October 1999 (latest figures available) there were 16,454 women in the Armed Forces, 
representing 7.9% of the total strength. 
 
 
 
 
 
                                                          
106 Fixed-wing pilots are pilots who fly fixed-wing aircraft. Aircraft are either fixed-wing (such as Jumbo jet, 
Concorde or Harrier [Harrier jump jet]) or rotary-wing (helicopters). 
 
107 Cap-badged posts are a means of identifying people from certain regiments and corps in the British Army to fill 
certain jobs. If you want someone who look after administrative jobs, then the post they fill will be cap-badged for a 
member of the Adjutant general’s Corps. Similarly, if you want someone who knows about firing guns and 
cannons, then the post will be for a member of the Royal Artillery.
