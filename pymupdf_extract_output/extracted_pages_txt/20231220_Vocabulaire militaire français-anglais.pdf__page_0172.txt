Organizing workshops 
 
Ladies and Gentlemen, 
 
Based on the outstanding results from the Operational Working Group that was held in conjunction with our 
Force Tracking Experiment (FTEX) at NC3A in February, and the ongoing work (and initial successes) with 
NATO-Nation Interoperability in ISAF, ACT will be conducting a follow-on Friendly Force Tracking (FFT) 
Interoperability Workshop. It will be held at JFC Brunssum in the Amsterdam Room 10 - 12 October 2007. Many 
thanks go to JFC Brunssum for agreeing to host this expectedly large gathering. 
 
The agenda is still in work, but the highlighted topics are: 
NATO-Nation Interoperability (NNI) in ISAF, including Lessons Learned 
NATO Friendly Force Information (NFFI) data standard 
Land Track Interoperability Service (LTIS) for future NATO operations 
FFT and Coalition Combat Identification (CCID) 
FFT and Air Command and Control 
National FFT Capabilities and plans for NNI 
NATO FFT Capability Development 
 
The calling notice (attached) is being sent to ACO and the National Liaison Representatives here at ACT. We 
wanted to make sure that all of you get it as soon as possible.  
 
CDR M. of the NATO HQ C3 Staff is requested to distribute to the C3 Reps.  
ACO is requested to distribute to your subordinate commands as you see fit. 
 
 
Also attached is an electronic version of the Registration Form to make it easier for you to register. Deadline for 
registration is 23 September, but since hotels in Brunssum are limited, we suggest that you book your rooms 
early.
