L3HARRIS TECHNOLOGIES
Business trends
	
 L3Harris Technologies revenues grew 9.8% in local currency in 2024, to $21.3 billion (€19.7 billion), driven in 
part by the full-year inclusion of Aerojet Rocketdyne. Higher sales of tactical and high-speed communication 
systems, as well as night vision systems, also contributed to revenue growth.
	
 L3Harris Technologies signed a letter of intent with Thales UK in May 2025 to develop a short-range air defence 
C2 system for the British Armed Forces, combining the group’s Target Orientated Tracking System with 
Thales UK’s Agile C4I at the Edge (ACE) system.
	
 The group inaugurated two extensions in 2025 at its production sites: in Fort Wayne, Indiana, in April and in 
Palm Bay, Florida, in August. These extensions required a cumulative investment of $225 million (€208 million) 
and will be used to produce missile warning satellites for the future Golden Dome missile defence system to 
be developed in the United States.
	
 In August 2025, L3Harris Technologies  began production of EMX series optronic and infrared systems at its 
new 2,000 m² factory in Katowice, Poland.
Home market
	
 The U.S. market is crucial for the group’s business (79% of revenues in 2024). The U.S. Department of Defense 
accounted for 76% of revenues (including Foreign Military Sales).
	
 L3Harris Technologies delivered its first OA-1K Skyraider II light armed ISR aircraft to U.S. Special Operations 
Command (SOCOM) in April 2025. The aircraft is expected to be operational by the end of 2026.
	
 Following an initial delivery in February 2025, L3Harris Technologies delivered a second Global 6500 business 
jet from Bombardier, modified for ISR missions, to the U.S. Air Force in July 2025. 
	
 The group received a $292 million (€270 million) order from Raytheon-Lockheed Martin Javelin JV in 
September 2025 to continue production of propulsion systems for the FGM-148 Javelin anti-tank missile for 
five years.	
	
	
	
Export markets
	
 21% of the group’s revenues came from exports in 2024, mainly from Australia, Canada and the UK.
	
 The group received two orders for its Falcon IV tactical radios in spring 2025: one from the Netherlands 
for a maximum of €1 billion in March (FOXTROT programme) and the other from Germany for $214 million 
(€198 million) in May (D-LBO programme).
	
 The Belgian Ministry of Defence ordered 14 medium-sized T4 mine clearance robots from the group in 
June 2025. These robots have already been sold to the United Kingdom and Australia.
	
 The group signed a contract with the Royal Moroccan Air Force in July 2025 to upgrade the avionics of C-130H 
Hercules transport aircraft (Lockheed Martin) by 2029.	
	
	
	
 In July 2025, the Italian Air Force ordered two EA-37B Compass Call electronic warfare aircraft from L3Harris 
Technologies for $300 million (€277 million). The group will work on this programme in partnership with 
BAE Systems (supply of the mission system).
	
 The Polish Air Force selected the group’s AN/ALQ-254 Viper Shield electronic warfare suite in August 2025 to 
upgrade its fleet of F-16 Fighting Falcon Block 52+ fighter aircraft (Lockheed Martin) to the Block 70 standard.
Technology and Innovation
	
 The group’s self-financed R&D expenditure amounted to $515 million (€476 million) in 2024, representing 2.4% 
of revenues.
