ABSTRACT  
The growing incorporation of unmanned systems (UAVs, UGVs, USVs, UUVs) within all domains is 
reshaping military operations, from planning to mission execution. NATO regularly demonstrates interest in 
training exercises such as REPMUS (Robotic Experimentation and Prototyping with Maritime Unmanned 
Systems), where the synchronization of hybrid manned-unmanned teams is critical. STAR (Sharing Tasks 
with Autonomous Resources) is an AI-driven tasking tool designed to share mission tasks across manned and 
unmanned systems, supporting planning, tasking, orchestration, and execution of coordinated multi-domain 
operations. STAR integrates multi-modal data, performs capability reckoning (assess capacity for 
autonomous and human agents), and delivers AI-based tasking suggestions to Command and Control (C2) 
systems with human oversight. Through modelling & simulation (M&S) environments like Unreal Engine, 
Cesium 3D geospatial modeling, and JSBSim flight dynamics, STAR enables high-fidelity mission execution 
including representative manned and unmanned vehicles with their appropriate assets and AI-based 
capabilities in realistic scenarios such as underwater protection, air-sea-ground combat, or search and 
rescue. STAR enables Man-Machine Interaction while allowing C2 operators to validate or override the 
tasking recommendation. Achieving tasking accuracy up to 89% using machine learning models, STAR 
shows promise to improve mission readiness and enhance coordination, interoperability, and 
synchronization of effects across joint multi-domain operations.
