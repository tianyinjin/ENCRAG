Regional metamorphism 
As affected by the regional metamorphism, the mining stratum generally generated 
the regional structural foliation (S1). There was a dip angle of 30～50° between the 
primary bedding surface (S0) and S1 (Fig. 5a). Obviously, such process might cause the 
fold deformation of syngenetic sedimentary ore-bearing bed.

Ductile shearing 
Some characteristic deformation structures in the ore district indicated the ductile 
shearing deformation in the late period, which also influenced the ore body shape. For 
instance: The thin marble and quartz vein were sheared and crumpled, eventually 
evolved to the rootless folding and lenticular body (Figs. 5b and c), which caused a 
false impression of “multilayer” of part stratum and ore-bearing bed (Figs. 5d and e).
