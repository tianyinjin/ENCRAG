Excuses/Apology 
Correction to CJOC Briefing and an apology from POLAD 
 
Colleagues, 
During the POLAD briefing on 4 Mar, I improperly read a headline from a news media article that referred to 
"Macedonia".   
 
The official NATO policy on this issue is clear.  The only appropriate terminology to be used within NATO is "the 
former Yugoslav Republic of Macedonia" which may by abbreviated as "FYROM".  (STANAG 1050) 
I sincerely apologize for giving offence to anyone who may have heard the briefing or been informed about it.  
This error was mine and does not in any way reflect any change of NATO policy on this issue, nor any intent on 
my part to express an opinion on the issue.  I regret any unhappiness this error may have caused. 
Jack S. (A6 - USA) 
Chief POLAD
