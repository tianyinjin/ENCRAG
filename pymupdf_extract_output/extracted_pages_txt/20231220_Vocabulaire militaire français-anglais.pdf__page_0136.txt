B R I T I S H  F O R C E S  
 
 
The Sovereign, as the head of state, is technically in charge of all the Armed Forces.  
In reality, Parliament is the decision-making body for defence. The Cabinet, and in particular 
the Secretary of State for Defence, is responsible to Parliament for these matters.  
The day-to-day running of the Armed Forces - in both peacetime and wartime - is the 
responsibility of the Defence Council, which is made up of members of Ministry of Defence and 
the Armed Forces.
