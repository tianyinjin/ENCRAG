Thank you to those who have replied to my email.   We have had a very positive response so far - the numbers 
are good.   If you have not replied yet please do so if you can.   However, please feel free to come whether you 
have replied or not, or even said no then changed your mind.   There is no seating plan. 
 
Food.   Plenty of snacks will be provided.   Everyone is requested to bring a pot luck dish, perhaps something 
national and festive.   Could 6 people please volunteer to bring a pudding dish (that's a sweet!).   Do come 
anyway, even if you don't bring something. 
 
Drink.   Drinks will be sold at the bar.   It's a pub - that’s how it works. 
 
Timings.   We will start from 1930.   Come any time after that. 
 
Directions.   A map will be distributed before the date.   There is plenty of car parking space. 
 
I will be away now until 3 Dec.  Please send replies to Simeon Godfrey in the CJOC next week, including 
volunteers for pot luck puddings. 
 
Yours 
 
Js
