Sayfa 2 
Haluk is a graduate of Middle East Technical University in Industrial Engineering and Columbia University 
in Financial Engineering. He is an avid fisher, frequently appears on Turkish television and at university 
conferences, but above all he enjoys spending quality time with his wife and three children. 
 
Medals and awards  
 
• 
Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky presented the Order of Merit Third Class to 
Haluk Bayraktar on 25 August 2020 for his contributions to elevating Türkiye’s 
relations with Ukraine to the strategic level. 
 
• 
Azerbaijani President Ilham Aliyev presented the Order of Karabakh to Haluk Bayraktar 
during his visit to Shusha on 15 June 2021 for his contributions to the territorial 
integrity and sovereignty of the Republic of Azerbaijan. 
 
• 
Haluk Bayraktar received the Antonov Medal on 14 September 2021 for his 
contributions to the aviation industry on the 75th anniversary of the Antonov aircraft 
factory’s establishment in Ukraine. 
 
• 
On 23 August 2022, Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky awarded the State Order 
of Merit First Class to Haluk Bayraktar for his major contributions to strengthening 
intergovernmental relations, promoting individual values and state sovereignty, 
Ukraine’s territorial integrity, and popularizing Ukraine worldwide. Mr. Bayraktar was 
the first Turkish national to receive this top-level honor in the history of Ukraine. 
 
• 
Ukraine’s Air Force Command presented Haluk Bayraktar with the Medal of Mastery, 
Honor and Courage on 6 April 2023. 
 
• 
Haluk Bayraktar was presented the Ordre de L’etalon Officier, Burkina Faso’s highest 
national honor, at a ceremony on 25 April 2023 by Grand Chancellor Sem Andre Rock 
Compaore, on orders from the country’s president, for his superior and exceptional 
contributions to Burkina Faso’s peace, security and fight against terrorism. 
 
• 
On 1 September 2023, Haluk Bayraktar received a medal from the Ukrainian Border 
Guards Command for his contributions to securing the country’s borders. 
 
• 
The President of Mali, Assimi Goita, awarded the National Order, the country’s highest 
honor, to Haluk Bayraktar on 18 October 2023 for his contributions and service to Mali. 
 
 
• 
On 28 October 2023, Haluk Bayraktar received the Daneker State Order from Kyrgyz 
President Sadyr Japarov for his contributions to the security and technological 
development of the Republic of Kyrgyzstan. 
 
• 
Haluk Bayraktar became the first civilian to receive the Medal of Superior Service, 
Kosovo’s highest military honor, from Kosovar Defense Minister Ejup Maqedonci on 6 
November 2023.
