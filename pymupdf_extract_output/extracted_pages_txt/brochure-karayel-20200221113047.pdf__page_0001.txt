Shrouded Small Scale
Wind Turbine
Shroud Geometry
Wind flow is accelerated by 1.4 times to the rotor axis via Shroud. This concludes that 
KARAYEL is able to generate 3 times power compared to same diameter conventional 
wind turbines.
Unique Design
KARAYEL is fully designed to fulfill customer’s requirements in our company.
No-Gearbox & Maintenance-free
KARAYEL uses direct-drive technology to reduce mechanical loses and become 
maintenance-free product.
Reduced Noise Level
Shroud represses tip vortices which is the main cause for aerodynamic noise. 
Additionally, mechanical noise is reduced immensely due to direct-drive technology, 
no-gearbox design.
Domestic Manufacturing
All KARAYEL’s mechanical components are being manufactured domestically.
