the intention of evaluating the counter-drone CV system on the basis of false alarm rate, missed detection 
rates, and classification accuracy and precision, and answer the finer questions asked by our leadership: “will 
our potential vendors’ models perform consistently in all-weather, all-terrain scenarios?”, and “will our CV
