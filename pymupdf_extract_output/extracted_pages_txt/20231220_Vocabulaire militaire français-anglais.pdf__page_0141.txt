P A R A D E  P R E P A R A T I O N  
 
 
Uniform will be BDU with Combat Jacket and Gloves. 
Transportation by bus. 
 
The group of Off’s and NCO’s are called separately  ”guard”. 
I will have the function of  “Guard Commander"  NCO’s. 
 
Each command at the parade will start with the warning command = “Guard !” 
For example: 
 
Guard - Attention 
Guard - Stand at ease 
Guard - Right /Left Dress (only heads turn, move till elbow distance) 
Guard - Eyes front 
Guard - Dismiss (turn right, start walking) 
 
Commands can be given by: 
Parade Commander = Gen Steltz 
Parade SM = CSM Oullette 
Guard Cdr  = OF4 (tbd) and me 
 
At the commands: Present Arms, Shoulder Arms, only the guard commanders and the colour 
parties will response. 
The guards (Off and NCO) remain in ‘Attention” position. 
 
General remarks: 
The gathering for the bus will be at 08.00.  
First a check if everybody is there, then departure by bus.  
With a little luck, coffee should be available during the training/rehearsal.  
If training goes well, it does not have to take as long as on the timetable. 
The parade itself will take about 2 hours, from 10.00 till 12.00. 
More details to be given by CSM Oullette. 
If you have any questions, please contact me. 
 
Thank you. 
 
Jos S.
