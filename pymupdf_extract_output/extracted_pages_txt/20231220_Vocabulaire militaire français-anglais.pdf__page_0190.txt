L A  N O T E  V E R B A L E  
Diplomatic note, note verbale 
 
 
 
 
No: PRB 10/614 
 
 
 
 
The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade presents its compliments to the Embassy of 
France and has the honour to refer to the Embassy’s Note No. 453 dated 25 October 2010 
nominating Captain Arnaud B. as Defence Attaché, to replace Colonel Philippe O. 
 
The Department has no objection to the appointment of Captain B. as Defence Attaché. The 
Department advises that Captain Bielecki should contact the Australian Embassy in Paris to 
obtain a diplomatic visa one month of the travel to Australia. 
 
The Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade avails itself of this opportunity to renew to the 
Embassy of France the assurances of its highest consideration. 
 
 
 
 
CANBERRA 
27 October 2010
