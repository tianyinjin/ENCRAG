Miratlas characterizes clouds and atmospheric turbulence 
to enable direct to earth laser communications regardless 
of weather conditions through optimal terrestrial site 
diversity and network routing.
ABOUT US 
Miratlas, characterizes clouds and 
atmospheric turbulence with a global 
network of Sky Monitors instruments of 
our own design and manufacture which 
rely on the passive analysis of the light 
from the stars and sun. Derived from 
astronomy, these high-precision and 
real-time data are critical for direct to 
earth laser communications.
Miratlas provides data to equipment 
manufacturers, satellites, and telecom 
operators. Enabling optimal network 
design and routing while ensuring the 
continuity and efficiency of operation 
regardless of atmospheric conditions.
OUR OFFER
Minimizing CAPEX: we optimize the 
design of the optical ground segment 
thanks to atmospheric data and 
modelling to inform: Optical Ground 
Station (OGS) design, number, and 
location to provide the best possible 
ground network design ensuring 
capacity, availability and maximizing the 
return on investment.
Minimizing OPEX: we forecast the 
laser link availability and capacity to 
optimize dynamic network routing to 
reduce handovers between decorrelated 
ground sites.
COMPETITIVE EDGE  
The Sky Monitor is the only instrument 
on the market providing continuous 
(night and day) and complete 
characterization of all the parameters 
impacting laser communication, 
absorption (cloud and water vapor) and 
scattering (turbulence).
Our global network of Sky Monitor 
provides continuous statistical data 
collection with the necessary climate 
and site diversity
