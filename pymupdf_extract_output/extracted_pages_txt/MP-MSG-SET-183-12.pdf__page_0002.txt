This paper presents findings from the field of small UAV detection. Other sequences of the engagement 
process, i.e. processing and elimination, are also - but only briefly discussed. The findings of the study [49], 
which has preceded this article, confirm hypotheses regarding the abilities to detect small drones. 
2.0 MATERIAL, EQUIPMENT AND METHODS 
Typical drones of specific categories were selected for to be tested in the experiments. Drones from mini and 
micro categories were chosen.  
For internal/laboratory experiments there were: 
• 
Octocopter 3D Robotics 3DR X8; 
• 
Quadcopter DJI PHANTOM 2 Vision;  
• 
Quadcopter DJI Phantom 4; 
• 
Hexacopter: 3D Robotics 3DR Y6; 
• 
Quadcopter DJI MAVIC Pro. 
In addition, for external experiments were selected:  
• 
Quadcopter DJI Inspire; 
• 
Hexacopter DJI S900, S800E; 
• 
Quadcopter TAROT F650. 
 
Figure 1: Examples of selected drones: a) 3DR X8; b) 3DR Y6;   c) DJI PHANTOM 2; d) DJI 
PHATNOM 4. 
The selection was based on sales analysis of Amazon and Techradar and on a flight parameter analysis, 
considering the flight time (operating time), the load capacity, and the teleoperation capability.
