I N V I T A T I O N S  
 
Sirs, Maams, Ladies and Gentlemen, 
 
Just to offer you a timely reminder that the UKDSU is holding a traditional British Summer Fete on Sun 30 Jul 06 
at the JFC BRUNSSUM Sport pitches (Kranenpool Industrial Estate), BRUNSSUM.  The Fete will open between 
1000hrs and 1600hrs and will include the following attractions : 
 
- Parachute Displays. 
- British and Dutch Brass Bands. 
- Helicopter Rides. 
- A variety of stalls from local businesses, including; car sales, craft and food stalls and toys and handicrafts. 
- Children's activities including; organised fun races, face painting, fancy dress and a bouncy castle.  (Prizes are 
available for many of these activities). 
- Static activities including; archery, laser sport, beat the goalie, a golf challenge and many more. 
 
A bar and BBQ will be open throughout the day, additionally ice creams and toffee apples will be available.  
 
The draw for the grand raffle will be made on the day and you have the chance to win some excellent prizes.  All 
money raised from the raffle will be split between local and British charities assisting disabled persons, children 
and the sick.  Tickets are priced Euro 1 and are on sale both now and on the day, so please donate generously.    
 
There really are too many attractions to list, so bring the family and come and support what promises to be a 
fantastic day out, you are all very welcome. 
 
Just in case I have missed anything important, I have attached a promotional poster for the event. 
 
Looking forward to seeing you all on the day, 
 
Respectfully, 
 
Paddy F., WO2 (OR8), UKA
