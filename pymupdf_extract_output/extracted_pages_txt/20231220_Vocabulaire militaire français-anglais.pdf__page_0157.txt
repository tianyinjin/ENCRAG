STAFF SUMMARY SHEET (SSS) (JFCB 35 – 4) 
Subject: 
APP-11/ADATP-3 - GEOGRAPHIC POSITIONS IN MESSAGES 
Tasker Tracker reference :Tasker-1623 
 
1. 
Purpose:    To provide COS with a letter to answer Encl 1. 
2. 
Conclusion:    There are no inconsistencies within JFC Brunssum or ISAF regarding the reporting of 
 
geographic positions.  
3. 
Recommendation:    That COS approve and sign Encl 2. 
4. 
Background:    In Encl 1, SHAPE explained that a recent meeting pointed out inconsistencies in 
geographic reporting procedures in NATO publications.  
5. 
Discussion:   J2 Division sees no inconsistencies within JFC Brunssum or ISAF regarding the reporting 
of geographic positions.  Reporting procedures for the ISAF and NRF missions are promulgated in OPLANs 
30302 Rev 1 (ISAF) and 30463 (SFJW 06) in APPENDIX 1 TO ANNEX T - Geospatial Support: 
…the designated horizontal datum and grid for the [ISAF or MVFOR] operation are The World Geodetic 
System 1984 (WGS-84), and Universal Transverse Mercator (UTM) grid / Military Grid Reference 
System (MGRS) respectively. 
This statement is in accordance with BI-SC Directive 80-4 "Position Referencing in NATO" and STANAG 2211 
"Geodetic Datums, Projections, Grids and Grid References." This STANAG provides a detailed explanation of 
how to correctly conduct position referencing.  We have not found UTM or MGRS to be “erroneous and 
inadequate” as asserted in Encl 1. 
6. 
This Staff Summary Sheet has been fully coordinated with J3
