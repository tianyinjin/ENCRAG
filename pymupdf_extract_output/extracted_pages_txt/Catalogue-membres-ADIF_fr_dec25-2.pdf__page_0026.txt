Description
LANDROVAL provides advanced EO/IR gimbals designed for tactical and ISR operations. Our
French-made gimbals (SCOPS, ELANION, ALUCO and NOCTULE) deliver real-time situational
monitoring through multi-sensor systems.
Our R&D teams are actively developing cutting-edge radio-frequency and acoustic detection
capabilities (ALUCO & NOCTULE) to ensure superior signal detection and localization with data
fusion performance.
Compact, rugged, and fully integrated with Jetson architectures, our solutions ensure
outstanding stabilization, precision targeting, and interoperability across unmanned platforms.
LANDROVAL is more than a manufacturer, we are a strategic partner committed to mission
success, reliability in the field, and European technological sovereignty.
When every second counts, LANDROVAL helps you see first, act decisively, and stay ahead.
