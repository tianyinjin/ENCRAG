Smartness 
 
1. Image : United States Navy personnel must set and maintain the high standards of 
smartness in uniform appearance. The military image reflected by attention to detail, 
while wearing your uniforms, is a key element in the public image of the Navy. 
 
2. Cleanliness : uniforms shall be kept scrupulously clean, with lace, devices and insignia 
bright and free from tarnish and corrosion. Shoes must be kept well shined and in good 
repair. 
 
 
Tenue (pour une invitation) : 
 
Just a reminder, the uniform's for the 40th Anniversary Events are as follows: 
Reception  (By Invitation Only) 
The uniform for the Reception is service dress for military and business dress for civilians.   
  
Celebration  (Open to all) 
The uniform for the Celebration is casual civilian dress (Collared shirt and slacks).  It is 
encouraged that personnel manning Delegation/Major Organization booths wear 
Traditional National/iconic dress (Examples:  Cowboy hat for Americans, Beret for French, 
Lederhosen for German, etc). 
 
Hope to see everyone there.
