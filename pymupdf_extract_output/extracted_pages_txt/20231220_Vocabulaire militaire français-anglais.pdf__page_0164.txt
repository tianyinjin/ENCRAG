_____ COMMON OPERATIONAL PICTURE 
 
Thank you for your letter of 2 Feb 07 outlining proposals for an expansion in the Common Operational Picture in 
____.  I agree that the maintenance of the highest possible level of Situational Awareness in an operational 
theatre is important.  However, there are considerations in this instance, which I believe are not completely 
conducive to a NATO-funded expansion programme. 
 
In consideration of the ____ operational necessity for the expansion of radar sensors in the North and West of 
____, our assessment of the current situation is that the degree of tactical air control requirements in the North 
and West is considerably lower than in the South and East.  Although the expansion of radar coverage within 
Regions North and West would certainly provide a great level of enhancement over the current situation, I 
assess that this currently falls above the threshold of what should be considered as the Minimum Military 
Requirement for conducting _____ operations in ____.   
 
Understandably, improving flight safety in ____ should always be a primary consideration and I appreciate your 
approach in suggesting enhancements.  Given the overall size of ____, we are breaking our assessment down 
by Regions and consider the potential overall value and benefit of radars in Regions North and West versus the 
overall ensuing cost.  Any additional input that you may wish to provide in order to inform our decision would be 
most welcome.  
 
I do not want this to seem as if I am closing the door on this issue, instead I offer you the opportunity to forward a 
detailed justification that would help us convince SHAPE as to why this requirement should be considered the 
Minimum Military Requirement.  Past experience has shown that highlighting the dangers of not including a 
capability vice listing its inherent benefits stands the greatest chance of success.   
 
I would also like to take the opportunity to mention that the NATO HQ-led Strategic Plan for the Normalisation of 
the ____ Aviation Sector in ____ deals with civil developments regarding additional radar sensors that may fit in 
the North and West of the country.  This is not NATO funded. However, if developed, it could also serve ____ 
purposes.
