Minutes Taking 
 
Sirs and Gentlemen, 
 
Let me just elaborate on the requirement here.  I am taking and writing up the minutes however, the value of this sort 
of conference is in the discussion which takes place inspired by the briefings.  To capture this discussion and record it 
is the task of the minute taker and to achieve that successfully rightly requires two people; the main minute taker and 
a back up who can take down key points and who is, ideally, more of a SME so that points are not missed through 
lack of understanding.  The minute taker (me) can then just pass the record of key points to the backup who can 
confirm that nothing has been missed.  This is hardly a difficult task and I am surprised at the debate it appears to be 
causing.  COS has had the position explained to him from the misinformation he was given last Friday at the COS 
mtg and agrees that this is sensible.  The requirement for a second minute taker therefore remains and I would be 
grateful for the names to be passed to J3 as requested below. 
 
I repeat, the main task remains with me and I will write the record.  The back up minute takers' task is to record the 
key points from his perspective and then agree with my record or add to it so that together the value of the discussion 
is captured.  The back up minute taker is only required during the topic for which he is the designated recorder. 
 
I trust that this fully explains to you the role and task of this function.
