Note No. TXS 027/02/11 
 
 
Protocol Directorate of the Foreign and Commonwealth Office presents its compliments to all 
Diplomatic Missions in London and has the honour to remind them of the procedures for 
security arrangements for VIP visitors to the United Kingdom. 
 
Diplomatic & VIP Security Section in Protocol Directorate is the first point of contact for all 
security matters relating to a VIP visit, including requests for protection.  It is essential that 
Missions give as much information as possible in good time, providing arrival and departure 
details, accommodation and the visit programme.  Details of any accompanying officials and 
security officers should also be provided.   This information must be sent to Diplomatic & VIP 
Security Section immediately (e-mail: ProtocolSecurity@fco.gov.uk or direct fax: 020 7008 
1026). 
 
Diplomatic & VIP Security Section is the contact point for radio frequency requests.  To clear 
a radio frequency for use during a VIP visit, the following information must be provided: 
bandwidth, frequency range (spectrum), preferred frequency and alternative, area of usage 
and power output.  
Missions are reminded that the import and use of electronic counter measures (ECM) or 
similar devices to jam radio frequencies is not permitted. 
 
Missions are also reminded that under United Kingdom law, the carrying of firearms, batons, 
Pepper and CS spray, tasers and knives by security officers is prohibited.  Any weapons 
brought into the United Kingdom must be surrendered to the appropriate United Kingdom 
port authorities on arrival and weapons will be securely stored.  Diplomatic & VIP Security 
Section must be given details of all weapons being brought into the United Kingdom so that 
the relevant port authorities can be notified in advance of the visit. Missions must also notify 
airlines or private charter companies, before departure, that weapons will be taken onboard 
the aircraft.   
 
Delay in providing the above information could hinder the Foreign and Commonwealth Office 
in meeting the Mission’s request for assistance.  It is Protocol Directorate's responsibility to 
liaise with the Metropolitan Police and other Agencies who may be involved security for the 
visit. 
 
Protocol Directorate avails itself of this opportunity to renew to all Diplomatic Missions in 
London the assurance of its highest consideration.
