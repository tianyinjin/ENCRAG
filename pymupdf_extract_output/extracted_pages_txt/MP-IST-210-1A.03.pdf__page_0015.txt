3.2 
Creating and Assessing Test Datasets for Static Perception Testing 
The objective of test dataset curation and creation is ensuring that a comprehensive mix of sensor, 
environmental, and target drone phenomena is represented to a sufficient degree to provide the end-user with 
assurance of sufficient test coverage for expected real-world phenomena. The data scientist’s judgement will 
have to be exercised during the curation of such a dataset, with a view to deciding on what images or videos 
would be relevant to the counter-drone use case.  
To that end, we curated a benchmark dataset, aggregating relevant data from our private drone video 
collection, the Anti-UAV300 [7] and Anti-UAV600 [21] datasets, and the multi-sensor drone detection 
dataset by Svanström et al. [16] as they were assessed to have representation of: defense or military contexts 
and imaging patterns (such as the cameras tracking drones overflying troops in the field), different relevant 
drone types, especially military-grade drones (such as the Geran-2 / HESA Shahed 136), flight profiles, and 
large corpus of infra-red sensors, with fields of view akin to real-world sensors deployed in ground-based 
drone defense settings, across differing environmental conditions. The initial benchmark dataset comprises 
two parts, designed to test CV models that would accept video input and/or single-frame input: a video 
dataset, comprising 121 self-collected videos captured under varying real-world UAV flight circumstances, 
and a video-derived image dataset, including both sampled frames from the videos and manually annotated 
single-frame captures, featuring diverse drone targets in a range of operational contexts.
