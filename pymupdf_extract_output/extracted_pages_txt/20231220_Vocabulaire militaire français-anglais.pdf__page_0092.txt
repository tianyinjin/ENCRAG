Network-Centric Warfare focuses “on the greater 
synergistic effect that can be created by networking and 
electronically linking geographically separated forces into 
one sensor-to-shooter engagement grid.” It provides the 
operator with enhanced situational and battle space 
awareness by extending the individual teams and 
network, allowing for access to a common operating 
Information Center, thus amassing information instead of 
combat forces, to effectively project combat power.
