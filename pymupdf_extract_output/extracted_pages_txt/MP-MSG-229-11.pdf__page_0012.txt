4.0 CONCLUSION 
Through the use of M&S STAR has been able to train and test different mission with an AI capable of 
reaching 72% [17] 89% [18] 88.1% [19]. This study demonstrated a proof-of-concept implementation for the 
integration of modeling and simulation into NATO’s force readiness framework, specifically in the context 
of MDO. The potential of such a tool is underscored by NATO initiatives like the Innovation Continuum, 
which seeks to advance Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance (ISR). Effective tasking and 
coordination of MUM-T remain significant challenges; however, the STAR system offers a promising 
solution by enabling comprehensive training and evaluation of MDO scenarios. This approach helps both 
manned and unmanned platforms identify operational strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for 
optimization. Looking ahead, future research will focus on refining STAR’s ability to integrate performance 
metrics for evaluating team effectiveness. By incorporating such metrics, the system can provide reflective 
feedback that drives continuous improvement, enhancing team efficiency and interoperability. In parallel, the 
STAR tasking algorithm will be further developed to enhance its predictive capabilities, enabling more 
accurate dynamic tasking and mission execution. The inclusion of expert feedback into the tasking process 
will foster a “teacher-student “ relationship between the operator and the system, promoting trust, 
transparency, and the explainability of AI-driven models which could evolve over time where the AI can be 
capable of assisting the human as a team member when human is overwhelmed or incapacitated due to 
cognitive degradation. This will ultimately strengthen decision-making in C2 systems, ensuring more 
informed and reliable operational choices to facilitate the MDMP and OODA loop. Additionally, research 
will explore the refinement of human meaningful control mechanisms within STAR, empowering operators 
to actively engage with and steer dynamic tasking scenarios through the recommendation algorithm. This 
balance between human vs AI decision-making is crucial for ensuring that the system remains adaptive to 
evolving operational needs. By enhancing the human-machine collaboration, STAR has the potential to 
significantly improve NATO’s readiness, enabling a more agile, resilient, and responsive force capable of 
navigating the complexities of modern, rapidly changing operational environments.
