2.1.1 
Modelling and Simulation 
Modelling and simulation enables answering several challenges for STAR. It first enables to simulate the 
actual mission operations, which involve many factors, actors and events. The first benefit is that it enables 
creating situational awareness data required for STAR to understand the operational context. This allows 
collecting multi-modal data (sensors, human, environment). The second benefit is that it allows co-hosting 
manned and unmanned agent systems which can both benefit from this simulated world where on one hand 
humans-in-the-loop can be immersed in a close to reality environment, which helps get human behavioral 
data such as performance, mental workload or stress which could be close to the real environment. Using 
M&S such as Unreal enables the creation of diverse scenarios such as search and rescue missions in water 
environment; ISR mission in MDO operations, or critical infrastructure operations such as NATO 
Experiment. On the other hand, it enables the autonomous vehicles such as UAV and UUV to be trained and 
tested in the same world or environment as human agent. Diverse plug-ins can be used to enhance the 
realism of the simulation and fulfil the need of data generation. Vigilante offers a platform for integrating 
manned and unmanned vehicles in realistic simulations, supporting a range of MDO operations ranging from 
air, ground, projectile and sea types of assets. These assets can be controlled via human operator within the-
loop simulation or can be completely autonomous. Based on the use-case, STAR can enable python scripts 
and different AI capabilities which can be integrated within those assets such as deep reinforcement learning 
algorithms, computer vision, path planning and more. This allows STAR to have realistic assets composed of 
battlefield capabilities used in operations. Secondly, one essential plug-in for the air domain is JSBSim. It is 
an open-source flight dynamics software used for realistic ranging from 737, B17, f-15, f-16 and more. It is 
widely referenced in both academic and industry research, with applications in reinforcement learning for 
flight control, as seen in studies like [15] and [16] (JSBSim on GitHub). Cesium provides 3D geospatial 
visualization, crucial for simulating large-scale operational environments and real-time geospatial awareness 
in MDOs. It can be integrated with game engines like Unreal for enhanced terrain modeling and operational 
planning (Cesium Unreal Plugin). Cesium World Bathymetry integrates bathymetric data, vital for naval, 
submarine, and amphibious operations, adding realism to underwater and coastal simulations (Cesium World 
Bathymetry). These tools collectively offer a comprehensive simulation ecosystem, essential for training, 
mission planning, and operational execution in modern military contexts.
