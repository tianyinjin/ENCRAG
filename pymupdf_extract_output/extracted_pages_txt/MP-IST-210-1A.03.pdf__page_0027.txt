4.1.3 
Insights into performance differences across scenarios 
Further, we also observe clear performance differences for the tested model operating under ideal conditions 
as compared to more challenging conditions, as we may observe further degradation in its drone detection 
capabilities in the presence of compression noise, differing brightness conditions, and detection of 
single/multiple drones as detailed in our test cases above. We further investigate its performance within 
different levels of these categories and arrive at the following results. 
4.1.3.1 
Performance differences between test cases of different background-light combinations 
From examining the data in Table 10, scenarios containing generally dark environments cause the CV 
system to perform poorly, with the lowest mAPs as a group ranging from 0.02 to 0.03 (sky/urban/foliage, 
dark), and at a generally high confidence level. Of the three environments, the foliage background also 
produces the worst results, with mAP ranging from 0.02 - 0.03. Notably, the CV system seemed to perform 
its best under (sky, bright), (urban, bright) scenarios, with mAP at 0.63 and 0.51 respectively with a 
confidence interval of 98% and above. This suggests that the CV system’s ability to perform under these 
conditions, even in the real-world, would be expected to be rather stable under ideal conditions, but may be 
prone to sudden and significant performance degradation should these conditions change (e.g. in an 
incoming storm, when clouds rapidly form and crowd out sunlight). A professional evaluation of such a 
system in our hypothetical scenario would consider the weather patterns, overall lighting conditions in 
various seasons within a temperature climate and design the appropriate safeguards and risk mitigation 
measures accordingly.
