If aphantasics lack modal memory but have personal memory and aﬀective mem-
ory (the other two aspects of episodic memory distinguished above), then these three
are not just diﬀerent aspects of the same kind of memory, but are two or three in-
dependent dimensions or kinds of memory instead. The three distinctions on the
amodal/modal dimension combined with the personal/impersonal memory contrast
results—at least hypothetically—in six diﬀerent kinds of memory, as shown in Ta-
ble 2.
