“Well guys, we’re at the church in the town and we must change our route !
First, we have to go northwards, along the street, over the bridge, and across the level crossing up to the
residential area. There, we have to keep going straight on to [tout droit vers] the coast and follow it until we find
the main railway line. We have to walk along it for about 2 kilometres, go under the motorway bridge and then
turn right to find a clearing [clairière].
Secondly, we have to go around the clearing and run southwards through the brushwood [brousailles] to the
main motorway junction ; and proceed [continuez] south-eastwards to the factory, bypass it to go up to a
crossroads on the road B3227. At this crossroads, we have to run across the open areas and along the path to
the river.
Thirdly, we’ll walk along its banks [rives] to reach and pass over a dam ; if it’s not possible, we’ll swim across
the river. Then, we have to move to the warehouses where a unit is waiting for us. Is that clear?”
