ATTENTION ALL ISAF HQ PERSONNEL
Reference: ISAF HQ SOP 231 (CJ2)
In the interest of ensuring that access to the ISAF HQ gym is controlled to eligible personnel and therefore
not over-used, effective Thurs 9 Aug 07, all personnel wishing to use the gym must present valid identification
pass for entrance and sign in on the gym register at the front desk. There is a notice at the front desk
identifying acceptable ID passes for admission.
In addition, the follwing gym rules will be strictly enforced:
No outdoor footwear to be worn inside
30 minute maximum use of aerobic machines, particularly treadmills
Garbage and water bottles to be deposited in appropriate receptacles
Room keys must be exchanged for borrowed (signed out) gym equipment to ensure its return.
Thanks very much for your cooperation. Questions may be addressed to Comd HSG.
