3. VOCABULAIRE AIR
The CFAS (airborne component of nuclear deterrence) and the CDAOA (missions related to the air defence
role) are part of the operational chain of command.
The CFAC (offensive and defensive force which ensures the defence of French national airspace), CFAP
(rapid and long range projection of forces to defend our country and protect its interests throughout the
world), CASSIC (possesses the surveillance and control [national early warning], ground-to-air defence
[defence of air bases that have a significant role in the airborne nuclear force, mainly] and electronic support
assets), CEAA (initial and further training of personnel throughout their careers) and CFPSAA (defence of
sensitive Air Force sites) are part of the organic chain of command.
The territorial chain of command includes two Air Regions (North and South) in which Air Force bases are
located.
