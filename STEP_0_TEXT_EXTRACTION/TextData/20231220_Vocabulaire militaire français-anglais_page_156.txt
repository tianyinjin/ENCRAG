We are very fortunate in the level of support available to call upon here in Brunssum. The UK
Delegation and Support Unit (UKDSU) provides a well equipped British Medical Centre, a supply
flight that provides not only Combat 95 etc but also a wide range of items from cash clothing. Our
UPO is Tri-Service and provides excellent support. Within the UPO we have a hardworking RN
element of POWTR Paulette K. and her sidekick WTR Matt B.. (PO K. has written to you
separately). Finally the UKDSU provides access to education and resettlement, a library, Post
Office, sport and AT, as well as administrating housing and accommodation for us all.
I must warn you that there is a lot of legwork involved in your joining routine when you arrive:
security procedures, ID cards, banks, driving licences, vehicle registering and so on. To guide
you through it all, PO (AWT) Spider K. will be your Sponsor. His telephone number in the HQ is x
3556. PO KELLY will in due course be in contact with you but do not hesitate to contact POWTR
K. or myself if you have any questions before you arrive. Additionally I strongly recommend that
you read the welcome pack that PO K. has sent to you and if you are able, access the UKDSU
website: www.brunsummbrits.com. Likewise www.rnrefernce.mod.uk has a link to the
overseas support unit (RNOSU), which gives good guidance for LFS drafts.
From previous experience, you may well be aware of the challenges, but also the immense
awards, of working in this multinational environment. NATO however is changing. The Alliance
has undergone a transformation that is both speeding up and changing the focus of what we do.
I am sure that it will not be possible, before you join, for you to attend the 1- Week NATO
SNCOs’ Orientation Course at Oberammergau, Germany. However we shall endeavour to get
you on this course from Brunssum: you will find it highly enlightening and beneficial.
In the meantime, we look forward to meeting you in December.
