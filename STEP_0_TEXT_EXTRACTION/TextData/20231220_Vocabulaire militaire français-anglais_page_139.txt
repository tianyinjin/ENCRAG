113 FLY-PAST AVRO LANCASTER
On 4 May 09, a Lancaster aircraft from the UK will overfly Brunssum as part of the Memorial Day commemoration.
Weather permitting, the flypast will occur at 1400 hours over the Lancaster memorial at Platanendreef 42 in
Brunssum (near where the road from Nuth passes over the N276 to Sittard). This will be a marvellous opportunity
to see one of the 2 remaining flying Lancaster aircraft make a rare overflight in The Netherlands and all JFCB
personnel are invited to watch the flypast. The Dutch authorities have been informed of the flypast and there is
likely to be significant interest from the local Dutch population. Our DCOM will be in attendance and JFCB
personnel are encouraged to attend this event in uniform.
David R P. - Gp Capt UK AF
