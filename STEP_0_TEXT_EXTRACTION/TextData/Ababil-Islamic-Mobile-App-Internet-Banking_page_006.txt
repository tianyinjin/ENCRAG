❖ Sign Up:

mobile phone number, and set password for secure login and the submit the form to the bank. In
admin module which will show all pending registration request and then customer will have to
come to the bank to fill out some paper forms and sign the agreement. Customer will need to
provide mobile phone’s IMEI number; each customer can include almost three mobile devices
